DeMajo

A mascot, its life in 3D and the cover art around a musician's releases.

Overview

I have worked with the musician DeMajo since 2017. With Bee Creative Group I created his mascot and the covers for 3 EPs and 2 albums. Since December 2025 I model and retouch the character and its props in 3D, and produce the renders and covers for his latest 4 singles and 2 albums.
